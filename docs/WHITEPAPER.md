# MiniTube Whitepaper 📜

## The Vision
The internet is dominated by algorithmic black boxes that sell user data, exploit creators, and extract maximum value while providing minimum transparency. MiniTube is the antithesis to Web2 media monopolies. Built natively on Farcaster's social graph, MiniTube is a decentralized media powerhouse that aligns the incentives of creators, consumers, and developers.

## 1. Current Phase: The Decentralized Social Layer
Our immediate priority is building a world-class vertical video experience without compromising on user experience or platform stability. 
- **Farcaster Native:** By leveraging Farcaster, we can aggregate decentralized feeds and provide an open social layer on top of existing Web2 video infrastructure (like YouTube).
- **Web2/Web3 Bridge:** We believe in bridging the gap seamlessly. The current platform acts as a powerful aggregator and curator, driving engagement through social mechanics rather than algorithmic manipulation.

## 2. Future Vision: Web3 Monetization
*Note: All features described in this section are currently in research and development and are NOT live.*

Traditional platforms take up to 45% of a creator's revenue. MiniTube plans to operate on a lean, transparent protocol model in the future.

### Smart Contract Tipping & Protocol Fee
In future versions, MiniTube will deploy a transparent routing smart contract. When a user tips 100 DEGEN to a creator:
- **97.5 DEGEN (97.5%)** is instantly routed directly to the creator's wallet.
- **2.5 DEGEN (2.5%)** is routed to the MiniTube treasury to sustain infrastructure costs.
This ensures the platform is sustainable without ever needing to sell user data or inject intrusive corporate ads.

### Non-Custodial Security
When tipping features are launched, MiniTube will never touch private keys or funds. Transactions will be passed directly to the user's native Web3 wallet. If MiniTube's servers are compromised, user funds remain 100% mathematically secure.

### Promoted Video Slots
Instead of algorithmic manipulation, creators will be able to transparently bid for visibility. Creators could pay a fixed fee in ETH/DEGEN to have their video guaranteed a spot in the first 5 swipes of the MiniTube feed. This will be an open market, verifiable on-chain.

## 3. The Future: $TUBE Tokenomics
*Disclaimer: MiniTube has NOT launched a token. Any token claiming to be MiniTube currently is a scam. This section outlines theoretical future utility.*

To further decentralize governance and align incentives, the $TUBE token is planned to serve as the native utility asset:
- **Discounts:** Paying for Promoted Video Slots using $TUBE provides a 50% discount.
- **Governance:** $TUBE holders will vote on major protocol upgrades, algorithm curation weights, and Treasury allocations.
- **Fair Distribution:** We believe in rewarding early believers. When the token launches, it will be heavily airdropped to users who have actively participated in the ecosystem.

## Conclusion
MiniTube proves that you can build highly addictive, world-class UI experiences without compromising on user sovereignty. By starting with a robust social layer and planning a future built on Ethereum's settlement layer, we are building the future of decentralized media.
