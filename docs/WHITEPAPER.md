# MiniTube Whitepaper 📜

## The Vision
The internet is dominated by algorithmic black boxes that sell user data, exploit creators, and extract maximum value while providing minimum transparency. MiniTube is the antithesis to Web2 media monopolies. Built natively on Farcaster, MiniTube is a decentralized media powerhouse that aligns the incentives of creators, consumers, and developers.

## 1. Absolute Trust & Data Sovereignty
At MiniTube, our philosophy on user data is simple: **We don't want it, we don't store it, and we definitely don't sell it.**
- **No Passwords:** Authentication is handled silently via cryptographic signatures through the Farcaster Frame SDK.
- **No Custody:** When a user tips a creator, MiniTube never touches the private keys or the funds. Transactions are passed directly to the user's native Web3 wallet. If MiniTube's servers are compromised, user funds remain 100% mathematically secure.

## 2. Monetization (The Web3 Way)
Traditional platforms take up to 45% of a creator's revenue. MiniTube operates on a lean, transparent protocol model.

### Smart Contract Tipping & Protocol Fee
In future versions, MiniTube will deploy a transparent routing smart contract. When a user tips 100 DEGEN to a creator:
- **99 DEGEN** is routed instantly to the creator's Farcaster verified wallet.
- **1 DEGEN (1%)** is routed to the MiniTube treasury to sustain infrastructure costs.
This ensures the platform is sustainable without ever needing to sell user data or inject intrusive corporate ads.

### Promoted Video Slots
Instead of algorithmic manipulation, creators can transparently bid for visibility. Creators can pay a fixed fee in ETH/DEGEN to have their video guaranteed a spot in the first 5 swipes of the MiniTube feed. This is an open market, verifiable on-chain.

## 3. The Future: $TUBE Tokenomics
*Disclaimer: MiniTube has NOT launched a token. Any token claiming to be MiniTube currently is a scam. This section outlines theoretical future utility.*

To further decentralize governance and align incentives, the $TUBE token is planned to serve as the native utility asset:
- **Discounts:** Paying for Promoted Video Slots using $TUBE provides a 50% discount.
- **Governance:** $TUBE holders will vote on major protocol upgrades, algorithm curation weights, and Treasury allocations.
- **Fair Distribution:** We believe in rewarding early believers. When the token launches, it will be heavily airdropped to users who have actively tipped creators through the MiniApp, proving their dedication to the ecosystem.

## Conclusion
MiniTube proves that you can build highly addictive, world-class UI experiences without compromising on Web3 values. By leveraging Farcaster's social graph and Ethereum's settlement layer, we are building the future of decentralized media.
